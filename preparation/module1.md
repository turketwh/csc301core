# CSC 301: Preparation Guide For Module 1

## Read And Watch
Read the following chapters of the *Course Reader* linked from *Course Administration* module of the course Canvas site. 

**By Friday, August 29, 2025:**

* Chapter 1: Strings And Biological Sequences
* Chapter 2: Strings And Biological Sequences: Activities Introduction

The major points to focus on in this part of the reading are:

* The idea of *strings* as sequences of *symbols* from an *alphabet*
* *Indexed* and *recursive* means of accessing symbols in a string
* The idea that different alphabets are meaningful in different problem domains

**By Wednesday, September 3, 2025:**

* Chapter 3: String Matching
* Chapter 4: String Matching: Activities Introduction

The major points to focus on in this part of the reading are:

* The definitions for *text* and *pattern*
* The general algorithm presented for finding instances of a *pattern* string within a *text* string
  * The general algorithm is presented in 3.3, followed by various implementations of that algorithm.

There may be a few videos linked in the reading that should be watched.  These will be annotated in a special callouts in the reading, each labeled as *Video Resource*.
